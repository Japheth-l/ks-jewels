Good to know

Questions & care

How do I place an order?

Add pieces to your bag, then tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order list opens as a ready-made message to KS Jewels — we'll confirm availability, payment and delivery with you directly.

What are the pieces made of?

Our collection features gold-plated jewelry and stainless steel pieces. Ask us on WhatsApp about any specific item.

Are your rings adjustable?

Many of our statement rings are open/adjustable. Message us with the piece you love and we'll help you with sizing.

How should I care for my jewelry?

Keep pieces dry — remove before showering, swimming or the gym. Put jewelry on after perfume and lotion, wipe with a soft cloth, and store separately so pieces don't scratch.

Do you do gifts?

Yes! Our gift sets come in the signature KS Jewels box. Tell us it's a gift when you order and we'll make it special.
